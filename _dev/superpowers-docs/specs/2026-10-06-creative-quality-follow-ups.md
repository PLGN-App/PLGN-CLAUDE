# Creative quality — follow-ups

## creative-quality leftovers, added 2026-10-06

- [ ] D1. `agents/plgn-creative-director.md`: Spec wording (D1.4) leaves an opening for the literal idea to slip back in. A single product frame is hero or result "unless the post is about the act of using it", and the Viola post (combing a girl's hair) can be read as exactly that. Add "a post about what the act achieves (no tears) is about the result, not the act" to close the loophole. Test by re-reading the rule and confirming the Viola case now rounds to result.

- [ ] D2. `agents/plgn-creative-director.md`: Small gaps in the director's text. The lens prefix being lower case is shown only by example, while the bold list is capitalised. "When the brief comes back for another round" does not repeat that the literal idea and anything under 6 stay out. Most lens examples are from hair-care (7:10 bus, strand, hairband, tube on the sink), so coffee or service brands may copy school-morning imagery. Add "in lower case" to the prefix sentence and "never the literal idea, never one under 6" to the round section; consider one lens example from another category.

- [ ] D3. `agents/plgn-designer.md`: Small gaps in the designer's text. "The command puts the palette in front of your text" should say "the brand's preamble". The seven parts give no guidance for type alone or a built object (lens feel, light and styling may not apply), and the accent rule may fight a type-alone layout. The 160-word cap includes exclusions and gives no order of priority; physical truth is never cut to meet the word count. Add one line for type alone (the shot is the type and its layout; skip lens feel and styling) and say that physical truth is never cut.

- [ ] D4. `skills/creative-brief/SKILL.md`: The "A real photo" bullet has an odd line break at line 92 (it left at the end), and says the real pack "is in the picture as ... result", but the director defines result as the pack absent or small. Re-wrap the bullet normally and write "in the picture, or behind it, as hero, detail, result or in use" or similar.

- [ ] D5. `skills/visual-identity/SKILL.md`: The palette row says the proportions are of "the brand's own things (the pack, the type, the set), not of the whole scene". For most shoots "the set" is the scene, so the example weakens the rule. The proportions sentence sits in the promptPreamble bullet in the art director, not in the `palette` bullet where the proportions are written. Consider "a set the brand built" instead of "the set" and consider adding the same half-sentence to the art director's `palette` bullet.
