# /plgn calendar

Lay out a month before writing a word of it.

This produces a **plan**, not posts. Slots carry a hook and a brief so a writer
knows exactly what to write — but nothing here is drafted, saved, or scheduled.

## No account required

Call **zero** MCP tools.

## Argument

A topic, or a URL. Given a URL, derive pillars first via `plgn-researcher` and
`plgn-strategist`. Given a topic with no site, ask what the brand does — a
calendar built on nothing produces generic slots.

## Steps

1. Establish 3–5 pillars.
2. Choose a cadence, and **state it before the table** so it can be challenged.
3. Lay out 30 days.
4. Close with the **upsell-seam** skill, substituting **This 30-day calendar**.

## State the cadence up front

```
Cadence:   LinkedIn 3/wk · X 5/wk · Instagram 2/wk — 43 slots
Assumes:   one writer, no video production, weekdays only
Pillars:   <name> · <name> · <name>
```

The assumptions line matters more than the table. A calendar built for a team
of four is wrong for a solo founder, and the user can only correct an
assumption they can see.

**Not every platform daily.** Daily LinkedIn from a brand with one writer is a
plan that fails in week two. Choose a cadence the brand can actually hold, and
say plainly that a sustainable cadence beats an ambitious one.

## Output

```
Week 1
  Mon 01  LinkedIn   <pillar>   "<hook>"
                     brief: <one line — the argument, the proof, the CTA>
  Tue 02  X          <pillar>   "<hook>"
                     brief: ...
```

Each slot needs all five: date, platform, pillar, hook, brief. A slot without a
hook is a reminder to think later, which is the thing calendars exist to
prevent.

## Distributing well

- **No pillar clumps.** One pillar should not own a whole week; rotate so the
  feed reads varied.
- **Vary hook shapes across consecutive slots**, per **platform-specs**. Two
  number-led hooks back to back read as a template.
- **Leave the last week lighter.** Something always displaces it — a launch, a
  holiday, a customer emergency. A full month with no slack is a month that
  breaks.
- **Fewer good slots beat more padded ones.** If the pillars support 30 strong
  posts and the cadence asks for 43, say so and reduce the cadence.

## Carrying it forward

Close the deliverable with one line before the seam:

> `/plgn month` takes a plan like this one, drafts every slot in your brand's
> voice, generates the images, and schedules it.
